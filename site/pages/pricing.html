Questions

Does my client need a ScopePay account?

No. Your client gets a private link to review the delivered work and accept it. They pay through PayPal, which accepts PayPal balances and, in most regions, cards.

Does ScopePay hold my money?

No. Invoices are created in your PayPal account and your client pays PayPal directly. ScopePay only reads the invoice status so it can close the milestone.

What if the AI gets a verdict wrong?

Every verdict shows the evidence and the clause it relied on, so mistakes are easy to spot. You can re-run the review after adding evidence, and the final decision is always your client's, not the AI's.

Which agreements work?

Any written agreement with deliverables and payment terms, as PDF, Word or plain text. You review and can edit every extracted milestone and condition before confirming.

What can I upload as evidence?

Screenshots (PNG, JPG, WebP), PDFs, Word documents and text files, up to 6 MB each.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from Settings in the app. Deleting your account removes your projects, evidence files and sessions.
