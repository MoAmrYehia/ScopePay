Privacy policy

Plain language, short, and specific to what ScopePay actually does.

Last updated November 8, 2026

Who we are

ScopePay is an independent beta product built for the PayPal AI Hackathon. It is not operated by PayPal.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, business name, email address and a hashed password.
  • Project content: agreements, extracted terms, evidence files, client names and emails, scope checks, approvals and invoice records you create.
  • Client decisions: the name your client types when accepting or declining, and the time.
  • Technical data: a session cookie to keep you signed in, and short-lived records of failed sign-ins to block abuse.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Why we use it

Only to provide ScopePay: to show you your projects, check delivered work against your agreements, let your client review and accept, and create and track your PayPal invoices.

Who else processes it

  • PayPal receives invoice details (your business name, your client's name and email, line items and amounts) to create and send invoices.
  • Our AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic, depending on configuration) receives agreement text, evidence and client requests to produce extractions and reviews. We send only what each task needs.
  • Render hosts the application and its database.

Your choices

You can edit your profile and delete your account at any time from Settings in the app. Deleting your account removes your projects, evidence files and sessions from our database. Invoices already created in PayPal remain in your PayPal account.

Demo data

The live demo creates a temporary workspace tied to a browser cookie. Demo workspaces that are not used for seven days are deleted.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on our GitHub repository.
