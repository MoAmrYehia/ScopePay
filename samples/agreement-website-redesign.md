# Website Redesign Services Agreement

**Between:** Jamie Lee Studio ("Designer")
**And:** Acme Studio LLC ("Client")
**Date:** October 6, 2026

## 1. Fees and payment

1.1 The total fee for this project is $4,500 USD, payable in three milestones.

1.2 Each milestone is invoiced through PayPal once the Client accepts the delivered work for that milestone. Invoices are due within 10 days.

## 2. Milestone 1: Discovery and design ($1,350, due October 20, 2026)

2.1 Sitemap covering every page listed in section 3.

2.2 Visual style guide with colour palette, typography and button styles.

2.3 High-fidelity desktop and mobile mockups of the Home page.

## 3. Milestone 2: Development ($2,250, due November 12, 2026)

3.1 Home page with a hero section, a featured work section and a "Book a call" call-to-action button.

3.2 About page with a team section showing each team member's photo, name and role.

3.3 Services page listing all four service packages (Brand, Web, Content and Growth), each with a starting price.

3.4 Portfolio page showing at least six case-study cards, each with a project image and title.

3.5 Contact page with a contact form (name, email, message fields) and the studio's office address.

## 4. Milestone 3: Launch and handover ($900, due November 26, 2026)

4.1 Site deployed to the Client's hosting with a working custom domain.

4.2 One 60-minute handover session covering content editing.

4.3 Written handover guide in PDF format.

## 5. Scope and change requests

5.1 The following are outside the scope of this agreement: blog or news section, e-commerce or online payments, copywriting of page content, logo design, and ongoing hosting or maintenance.

5.2 Any work outside the scope in 5.1 or beyond the deliverables in sections 2 to 4 is a change request. Change requests are estimated in writing and billed at $95 per hour, and work begins only after the Client approves the estimate.

## 6. Acceptance

6.1 The Client reviews the delivered work for each milestone within 5 business days and either accepts it or lists the deliverables that do not meet this agreement.

6.2 Acceptance of a milestone is recorded in writing (including by electronic approval) and triggers the invoice for that milestone.
